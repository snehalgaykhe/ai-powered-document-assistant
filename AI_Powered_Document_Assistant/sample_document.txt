DEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE: COMMUNITY TECH SUPPORT

Support hours:
The support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, excluding public holidays.

How to request support:
Users should email support@example.test with a short description of the problem, the device or application affected, and any error message.

Escalation process:
If a critical service is unavailable for more than 30 minutes, mark the request as urgent and contact the service coordinator. For non-critical requests, the support team aims to respond within two business days.

Troubleshooting:
Before submitting a request, restart the affected application and check the network connection. Include the steps already attempted when contacting support.
